On cherry-picked noise a lower pass rate is better; on the planted edge a higher one is. On these two cases the tools tie at their top grade: neither validates cherry-picked noise and both validate the planted edge. They differ one level down. overfit-gauntlet calls all 40 noise picks WEAK, its middle grade; bt-audit grades 38 of 40 FAIL and 2 WARN. bt-audit's overall grade is its weakest check, so a true edge can still miss PASS on costs or sizing; the DSR and SPA columns isolate the selection-bias checks. overfit-gauntlet is about twice as fast per run.

An unlabelled file being flagged is not proof of a false positive; it is a file to read.

## What this does not cover

overfit-gauntlet runs checks bt-audit does not (sign-permutation and bootstrap-vs-zero tests, walk-forward, purged k-fold), and bt-audit runs checks it does not (PBO via CSCV, Hansen SPA, bootstrap drawdown and ruin by leverage, a correlation-aware Deflated Sharpe). Only overlapping questions were compared.
